What the academy has to do

  1. 1

    Sign internship agreements before students need them

    Target the six companies above. A written internship memorandum with Uzum, TBC and Hamkorbank covers most of an SL cohort.

  2. 2

    Keep a live vacancy board

    Track hh.uz, LinkedIn and Telegram channels weekly. A student finishing SL should see real openings that match what they just learned.

  3. 3

    Build the stack the market asks for, not the one we prefer

    Every listing above wants PostgreSQL, Docker and Git. These belong in the curriculum because employers name them, not because they are fashionable.

  4. 4

    Teach the interview, not just the job

    CV review, LinkedIn profile, algorithmic practice and a mock technical interview inside FO. Most rejections happen here, not in the work itself.

  5. 5

    Run an alumni and hiring-partner channel

    A graduate inside Uzum is the best recruiter for the next cohort. Keep them connected and ask them to refer.
